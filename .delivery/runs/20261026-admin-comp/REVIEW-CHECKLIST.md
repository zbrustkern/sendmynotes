# Founder staging review — admin complimentary cards

Use the staging admin panel after signing in. Keep ordinary production sending separate from this review. Staging is deliberately held: recording an order here does not collect payment, email anyone, or submit a card to Handwrytten.

1. Open the admin complimentary-card composer. Choose a cover, write a short note, enter fictional recipient details and a personal US return address for this test. Confirm no studio address or promotional note is silently inserted.
2. Choose US and Fully complimentary. Review the $9 list price and $0 customer total; provide a reason; go back and confirm inputs survive. Record the staging hold once. Confirm the outcome says held, not mailed.
3. Repeat with an enabled international destination. Confirm country-aware region/postal fields and the separate $5 upgrade. Fully complimentary should cover the entire $14, leaving $0 due.
4. Choose individual adjustments. Waive $9 of the card line and leave international mailing unwaived: $5 remains due. Alternatively waive $2 domestically: $7 remains due. Review clearly separates list price, admin adjustment and balance.
5. Record a partially adjusted staging order. Confirm it stays held and does not present a real payable link or imply payment was collected. Both full and partial admin-origin records are excluded from independent purchase measurement.
6. Try an empty reason, incomplete sender, invalid/excess waiver and a changed destination. Errors should preserve the card and addresses and require a fresh quote/review where appropriate.
7. Verify the dialog at mobile width and with keyboard-only navigation. Close/back/review/retry should be understandable; repeated submission should not duplicate the accepted order.

## What this review does not establish
No future add-on product is enabled; automated injected-catalog tests prove the pricing extension. Provider-backed Stripe payment, a real complimentary Handwrytten order, printed envelope accuracy, actual supplier costs and delivery remain separate live validation gates. The positive-balance capability/payment flow is exercised using fake provider adapters, while guarded staging stays nonpayable. Production rollout is not part of this release.
