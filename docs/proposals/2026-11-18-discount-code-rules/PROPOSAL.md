# Discount codes for multisend, international mail and wedding projects

November 18, 2026 · Product and engineering proposal

Extend the SendMyNotes offer engine so an operator can specify **who qualifies, which charges receive the discount, and the most the offer can cost**. A sender can enter a code or open a link exactly as they do today. The server applies the same published terms in either case. Keep pricing and promotion decisions in SendMyNotes, where recipient destinations, approved wedding cards, and project purchase history are known. Send the resulting payable total to Stripe.

## How an operator would define an offer

| Decision | Rules to support |
| --- | --- |
| **Which purchases qualify?** | Choose the product or workflow, price schedule, eligible recipient countries, minimum eligible card count or subtotal, and time window. Name the counting period explicitly, such as this order or purchases in this wedding project. Compute minimum spend on eligible pre-promotion charges, excluding tax. |
| **Who may redeem it?** | Choose anyone, a verified sender, or an authorized member of one named project. A campaign may issue many private, random codes under the same terms and budget. Each code can narrow the audience, expiry or use count. A public code can work for guests. |
| **What gets discounted?** | Select a charge such as card base or international surcharge. Choose a fixed amount once per order, an amount per eligible card, or a percentage of eligible charges. Set the maximum discounted units and cents. For limited-unit offers, specify a deterministic selection rule, such as earliest eligible project positions. |
| **How far can it go?** | Set limits per order, sender, project, issued code and campaign as appropriate. Track redemptions, discounted card count and subsidy in cents separately. One redemption might represent 25 cards. Codes and revised terms draw on the same campaign budget. |

Use a small registry of typed, server-supplied facts and charge types. Combine requirements with AND and permit lists within a field, such as Canada or the UK. Keep product, send mode, recipient country and charge type independent. For future mixed-destination baskets, distinguish “all recipients must qualify” from “discount matching recipient lines only.” Unknown facts make a restricted offer ineligible, or provisional while the sender is editing. Registering a future paid feature makes it available for an operator to select. Existing policies retain their published allowlists.

Use one promotional code per order initially. Give each price schedule an explicit eligibility setting. Ordinary offers exclude the wedding pilot. A wedding-specific offer can expressly apply to its actual graduated prices. Keep tax outside the discount targets and pass the resulting allocations to any applicable tax calculation. Keep zero-dollar outcomes an explicit offer permission within supported checkout flows. Reject or cap offers that would leave a positive balance below the applicable processor minimum, and show the resulting terms before payment. [Stripe charge minimums](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies#minimum-and-maximum-charge-amounts)

## Worked examples

These are illustrative terms and USD totals before applicable tax. The wedding case uses the proposed domestic pilot rates.

| Example | Intended purchase | Boundary the operator can preview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Domestic multisend** · 20% off eligible card bases on 5+ cards, up to $9 per order | Five cards cost $45 and pay **$36**. Eight cost $72 and pay **$63**. | Four pay $36 at regular price. A single card or wedding batch receives no benefit. Add a verified-sender campaign limit if this offer should work only once across separate orders. |
| **Canada mailing** · $3 off one Canada surcharge | An enabled Canadian single costs $9 + $5 and pays **$11**. | A US single pays $9. A disabled destination stays unavailable. A destination still awaiting entry can display the terms and confirm savings when the address qualifies. |
| **Private wedding credit** · $1 off each of the first 25 domestic cards purchased in one named project, including its graduated schedule | Fifty cards have a proposed subtotal of $377.50 and pay **$352.50**. If the couple buys 20 and then 30, the discounts are **$20 and $5**. | The other project owned by the same sender receives $0. Held cards earn no positions. Resending a replacement for a purchased card consumes no additional benefit. |
| **Future design add-on** · a credit on a named paid design feature | If the feature later has a $4 fee, a $4 add-on credit reduces that line to **$0**. | With no eligible fee, savings are $0. No unused credit flows to the card or mailing charge. |

## Checkout and redemption

1. **Build one priced basket on the server.** Give each selected card a stable ID and separate amounts for its card base and any surcharge or add-on. Derive workflow, destination, project membership, paid history and unit price from validated records. A landing page, campaign tag or typed email can suggest a code. Identity-restricted offers require a verified account or verified email link. A project restriction also requires project membership. These controls bound the benefit per verified identity. Keep a campaign-wide ceiling even when one sender can have several identities.
2. **Preview with the checkout evaluator.** Return eligible lines, allocated savings, amount due and a short explanation. Entering a code keeps inventory available until checkout starts. Changes to destination, approved selection, quantity, identity or project revision require a new quote. Show the new total for confirmation before payment. A basket can qualify for an offer while some of its charges remain payable.
3. **Reserve the exact exposure.** In a Firestore transaction, require an active offer and check that redeemed subsidy + live reserved subsidy + this quote's subsidy fits the budget. Apply the same arithmetic to uses and discounted units at each relevant scope. Record the quote, owner, currency, pricing and policy versions, cart revision, allocations and expiry. Reserve wedding tier positions and remaining project credit consistently across concurrent batches. When funds are insufficient, decline the offer unless a smaller, explicitly advertised benefit applies. Keep payment and supplier calls outside the transaction because Firestore can retry it. [Firestore transactions](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/manage-data/transactions)
4. **Consume once and reconcile payment outcomes.** Bind the PaymentIntent to the order and quote. Verify capture amount and currency against the frozen total, then consume the matching active hold once across confirmation and webhook delivery. For an authorized $0 order, commit its redemption before releasing it for dispatch. Allow only the checkout owner or a trusted server process to change a hold. At expiry, close or reconcile the associated payment before returning capacity. A payment captured on time and reported late keeps its quoted terms. An ambiguous payment enters reconciliation before another attempt can spend the same capacity. [Stripe webhook delivery](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks#handle-duplicate-events)
5. **Refund what each card actually paid.** Allocate a fixed order discount proportionally across eligible charges in integer cents, with stable tie-breaking for leftover cents. A surcharge-only offer allocates solely to its eligible surcharge. Store gross, allocated discount and net by line, and require their sums to match the payment. Use the frozen net allocation for partial refunds. Preserve the current default that a refund does not restore promotional usage. Track replacements against the original delivery. [Existing multisend refunds](../../../lib/multi-send/refunds.ts) · [Existing refund policy](../../../lib/offer-engine.ts)

## Admin and rollout

In the existing Promotions workspace, offer presets for single card, domestic multisend, destination surcharge and private wedding project. Before publishing, show a plain-language summary, a qualifying basket, a near miss, the largest permitted basket, and the maximum subsidy per order and campaign. Show “unbounded” anywhere a total has no configured ceiling. Require explicit budgets for private credits, free cards and offers that multiply per card. For wedding offers, show the lowest eligible unit price after the discount so an operator can judge the economics of later tiers. An operator can pause new reservations, see held and spent amounts, and inspect which rule caused a decline. Limit repeated public code attempts and keep private codes out of analytics. Use the existing audit trail for publication, pause and reissue.

1. **Make existing promises enforceable.** Inventory active public and issued codes, including post-purchase grants, and identify which codes are transferable. Map legacy fixed and percentage amounts to one $9 base benefit per order across currently supported flows. Preserve their existing batch allocations, surcharge exclusions, historical counts and in-flight quotes. Review active codes with advertised identity limits before enforcing an identity requirement on returning guests. [Post-purchase grants](../../../lib/post-purchase/offers.ts)
2. **Release scoped multisend and international offers.** Introduce the basket evaluator in comparison mode and check old and new totals across single, international, multisend and free checkout. Switch one bounded campaign first. Store new policy versions on publish and route old pending orders through their original quote version. Expose each admin control only when every applicable redemption path enforces it.
3. **Enable private wedding offers with the paid project ledger.** Issue a code bound to one project, serialize overlapping purchases against the same tier positions, and apply any approved credit after graduated pricing. Set subsidy caps from an explicit pilot budget. Bulk supplier terms and contribution remain assumptions until reconciled with actual charges. [Wedding pilot economics](../2026-11-12-thank-you-portal/PRICING_RESEARCH.md)

**Release checks.** Use synthetic baskets and test payments. Check the four worked examples, a forwarded private code, a destination edit, two simultaneous checkouts for the last dollars, duplicate payment notifications, an expired hold with a late capture, paid and free orders, an in-flight quote across a policy edit, split wedding batches, and a partial refund. The last $5 of a campaign can fund at most one $5 reservation. Every refunded delivery must reconcile to the amount originally allocated to it.

## Precedents for the design

- Stripe lets a customer-facing code refer to an underlying promotion and narrows its use by customer, expiry and redemption count. This supports issuing separate codes for individual couples under one campaign. [Stripe promotion codes](https://docs.stripe.com/api/promotion_codes/create)
- commercetools distinguishes the conditions that qualify a cart from the effect on items, multibuy units, shipping or the total. It recalculates on cart changes. That distinction lets SendMyNotes qualify a purchase by country while discounting either the card or the surcharge. [commercetools cart discounts](https://docs.commercetools.com/learning-price-and-discount-your-products/cart-discounts/how-cart-discounts-work)
- Voucherify supports campaign ceilings on total discount amount and redemption count, together with customer and code limits. A shared monetary budget gives SendMyNotes a defined maximum exposure even when several private codes circulate. [Voucherify campaign controls](https://www.voucherify.io/incentive-governance)
