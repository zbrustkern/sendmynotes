## International addressing and envelope names

Use the same country picker, aliases, region choices, postal requirements, suspension list, payment-mode gates and supplier country IDs as single-card sending. If service changes afterward, hold the affected paid card for resolution or refund at its frozen amount.

Show a country-appropriate form and formatted envelope. Canada uses province and postal code; the UK uses a postcode and does not require a US state. Preserve diacritics, international spacing, leading zeros and optional fields. Do not apply US-only postal validation or address corrections overseas. Label the evidence accurately (sender confirmed, structurally complete, provider checked or needs help). Unknown, ambiguous, suspended or incomplete destinations stay editable while the rest of the project moves on. A sender can finish the wording or request an address for any held row. Show **+$5.00 international mailing** as soon as the destination is known. Before charging, show the exact included and held counts. Domestic and international deliveries have separate estimates; international timing varies by country and local postal service. Recheck the catalog against supplier data and [USPS suspensions](https://about.usps.com/newsroom/service-alerts/international/) before enabling staging acceptance.

**Supplier mapping.** Preserve the original envelope label verbatim. Store sender-confirmed supplier *first* and optional *last* components alongside it; render the exact joined components for final review. For already structured names, keep both fields. For a single free-form or collective label, propose the complete label in the first field with last empty; show the resulting envelope and allow explicit edits or a sender-chosen split. Never infer a surname from the last token, substitute “Friend”, or borrow the inside greeting. Example fixtures: “The Patel-O’Connor Family”, “Dr. & Mrs. Maya and Jordan Patel”, a mononym, “Alex & Jamie Rivera” and “Alex Patel & Jamie Kim” as return names. Require a nonempty personal sender first component. Proposed application bound is 80 Unicode code points per component; preserve overlength originals and ask for an approved shorter mailing label. Verify supported lengths, blank-last and joint rendering for the SKU before live release. No silent truncation.

Handwrytten documents separate first/last fields, recipient country ID, ZIP, and state for US/Canada. The single-step schema marks recipient ZIP required. If a destination normally has no postcode and no approved supplier representation exists, ask for review and hold that row; never fabricate a code. The API does not specify a reliable envelope name-field maximum or physical joint-name wrapping. Keep these as named supplier verification items; use existing sample evidence or read-only documentation. ## Note modes and approval

**One project, mixed use.** Alex writes “Thank you for thinking of us on our wedding day. We’re so glad to have you in our lives.” The first card opens with “Dear Noor and Kit,” and the second with “Dear Anaïs,” on its own line. Alex can replace just Noor and Kit’s body with “We made your lemon pasta in the blue bowl on Tuesday. Thank you for both the bowl and the recipe.” Editing the shared note later leaves that personal body intact.

| Choice | Resolution | Approval rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shared note with names** | Compose the sender’s shared body and sign-off with each household’s confirmed greeting. Offer joint names, an explicitly chosen general greeting or an explicitly blank greeting. Display the exact shared body once, the complete list of resolved greetings and exceptions, and a full-card preview for any row. | One explicit **Approve shared note for N selected households** action. Show who is included. Resolve each row to an exact revision on the server. Missing or ambiguous names need the sender’s choice. No 100 identical body approvals. |
| **Personalize this card** | Start from that household’s resolved note. Edits to greeting, body or sign-off become field-specific overrides. Indicate which fields still use shared defaults. **Use shared wording again** shows the replacement and asks before discarding an edited body. | Review the complete customized note. Approving shared wording excludes customized bodies awaiting review. Moving between modes preserves existing personal notes. |
| **Help me draft** | Offer suggestions for one household or selected blank notes using its confirmed gift, supplied memory and chosen tone. Preserve imported or typed messages verbatim until the sender accepts an edit. Keep generated suggestions separate from sender text. | Each distinct generated message must be shown in full and accepted. No silent bulk approval of unseen AI text. Never invent attendance, relationships, gift use or cash amounts. An unavailable model leaves writing and named batch-send usable. |

**Reusable wording.** Start with one shared note; optionally create up to four named templates. Couples can apply a template to a filtered, explicitly selected group, see who will change, and undo the assignment. Never infer attendance, link a cash amount, or assume a relationship from a gift row. A template revision affects only inheriting fields of households assigned to that template. Preserve individual overrides, exclusions, pending suggestions and paid records. The shared approval action applies to one exact template revision and its displayed membership.

**Will my words fit?** Count the final composed greeting, separators, body and sign-off with the same normalization and JavaScript string-length rule as checkout. Display remaining capacity as the sender writes, including emoji and line breaks. At 451/450, keep the words, flag the specific card and link directly to its editor; 450 passes the structural check. Before a shared greeting/body/sign-off edit is applied, preview the number and names of cards pushed over the limit. Hold those cards from purchase until corrected. The 450-unit cap is the application limit; line wrapping and physical fit vary by handwriting and need supplier-backed evidence. The supplier FAQ describes a separate 50-character wishes field, while our integration sends sign-off inside the composed message. [Supplier FAQ](https://www.handwrytten.com/about/the-company/).

The greeting resolver uses a confirmed inside name or literal greeting from that row. The envelope name, the personal return-address name and the signature are independently editable. A named salutation copied from the first recipient, unresolved placeholder or another household’s private detail blocks bulk approval until reviewed. Treat an intentional blank as different from a missing greeting. Changing shared text reopens only approvals inheriting that field. Changed recipient identity or greeting reopens the affected note. Changing only a street/unit triggers envelope review and preserves approved wording. Paid snapshots stay immutable.

**Blend with existing multi-send.** From an unpaid general batch offer **Continue as a thank-you project**. Show the count and the fields to be carried over. Copy design, handwriting, sign-off, return address, recipients, explicit greetings and shared body into a new owned project once. Preserve blank and custom greetings. Flag inherited named greetings for resolution. Leave the original draft recoverable. If it has a pending intent, settle or confirm cancellation before allowing the same batch to be purchased through the project. A paid order opens its status. A couple starting inside the wedding portal gets the same familiar shared-body interaction without a detour through general checkout.

## Financial contract

**Customer promise for the pilot.** All displayed wedding card rates and the **$5.00 per international card** mailing surcharge are tax-inclusive USD prices. The displayed total is the amount charged. Domestic mailing is included; no extra tax is added at checkout. Use the same amounts in the estimator, selection summary, pay button, confirmation, receipt and refund view. Current Stripe PaymentIntents take an amount and currency; the pilot does not calculate or separately itemize sales tax. Keep the accounting tax amount **unknown/unassessed** until a tax calculation exists. An all-in price alone does not establish a tax rate, exemption or remittance amount.

Price each physical card by its **position among all purchased envelopes in this project**, irrespective of destination: 1–10 at $9.00; 11–25 at $7.50; 26–99 at $7.00; 100 onward at $6.50. Apply the existing `INTERNATIONAL_SURCHARGE_CENTS = 500` exactly once to each eligible non-US envelope. Domestic and international cards earn identical ordinal credit. The $5 never receives a tier discount. Drafts and holds earn no ordinals. Refunds and supplier-funded replacements do not create or reclaim tier positions. General checkout offers retain their own behavior; disclose the wedding schedule before payment.

## Preparing and settling large batches

The current multi-send creates children with sequential transactions, the refund path reads every sibling, and the cron processes one parent and up to five children per invocation. A 100-card order needs a separate scalable path.

1. Reserve the ordered households once and enqueue preparation. Write deterministic manifest chunks in bounded, retryable batches (initially 20 children per chunk) using hashes, stable IDs, expected counts and generation IDs. Report preparation progress and allow the user to return. A crash or retry resumes missing chunks. A short final transaction verifies the chunk completion markers, hashes, reservations and totals and commits a ready-to-pay marker. Create/reuse the Stripe intent outside the transaction with an idempotency key bound to that marker. These prepayment records are immutable quote lines. Only verified settlement may authorize post-payment delivery records. Keep bulk writes and supplier calls out of request/webhook transactions.
2. On verified capture, match the frozen quote, intent, amount and currency, update the parent/ordinal ledger once, and enqueue idempotent follow-up. Show the held purchased envelopes in staging. A webhook replay, concurrent tab, expired reservation or late capture has a deterministic reconciliation path. Never reopen an uncertain intent merely because a lease expired.
3. A child refund reserves against its own frozen refundable components plus parent captured/refunded/reserved counters in a bounded transaction. Complete or release the reservation using a unique refund key and processor evidence. Neither refund requests nor webhooks must read all siblings. Reconcile aggregates asynchronously against child/Stripe evidence.
4. Use a fair queue over eligible parents, bounded child concurrency, time-bounded leases, per-child idempotency and backoff. Quarantine uncertain supplier acceptance for operator reconciliation. Test a large and small project interleaved. Record chunk timing, documents read/written per refund, queue age, fairness, retries and configured limits. Compensating operations keep the captured snapshot and paid ordinal history intact. In staging use held orders and an isolated mock dispatcher; supplier-authorized throughput, scheduler capacity and verified international supplier costs are live-release gates. [Firestore bulk-write and contention guidance](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/best-practices).
