We’re so glad to have you in our lives.” The first card opens with “Dear Noor and Kit,” and the second with “Dear Anaïs,” on its own line. | **Help me draft** | Offer suggestions for one household or selected blank notes using its confirmed gift, supplied memory and chosen tone. **Reusable wording.** Start with one shared note; optionally create up to four named templates. Couples can apply a template to a filtered, explicitly selected group, see who will change, and undo the assignment. Preserve individual overrides, exclusions, pending suggestions and paid records. The shared approval action applies to one exact template revision and its displayed membership.

**Will my words fit?** Count the final composed greeting, separators, body and sign-off with the same normalization and JavaScript string-length rule as checkout. Treat an intentional blank as different from a missing greeting. Changing shared text reopens only approvals inheriting that field. Changed recipient identity or greeting reopens the affected note. Changing only a street/unit triggers envelope review and preserves approved wording. Paid snapshots stay immutable.

Define a replaceable `TaxCalculator` between pricing and payment. A future policy can allocate tax **within** the listed price or **add** tax after explicit display/reconfirmation. Configure sourcing, taxability of base and mailing, registrations and rounding per jurisdiction; a multi-destination batch may require separate calculation groups. Freeze provider/version, tax basis, results, expiry and allocated cents with the quote. Record tax transactions only on settled payment and reverse allocated components on refund, idempotently. A required calculator outage or expired result triggers retry/requote before payment. Historical receipts and refunds read their original snapshots. Enabling a future provider is a configuration, tax-policy and adapter release; existing paid orders stay readable. Verify modes with a synthetic 10% rate: on a $9 card, inclusive gives $8.18 net + $0.82 tax = $9.00; added gives $9.00 + $0.90 = $9.90. Neither example is a live rate. [Stripe Tax calculations and reversals](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/standalone-tax-api).

## Preparing and settling large batches

The current multi-send creates children with sequential transactions, the refund path reads every sibling, and the cron processes one parent and up to five children per invocation. A 100-card order needs a separate scalable path.

1. Reserve the ordered households once and persist an indexed Firestore preparation job. While review is open, the client calls an authorized POST that advances one leased chunk of at most 20 children. Persist each chunk before returning, using bounded writes with hashes, stable IDs, expected counts and generation IDs. Report preparation progress and allow the user to return. A crash or retry resumes missing chunks. A short final transaction verifies the chunk completion markers, hashes, reservations and totals and commits a ready-to-pay marker. Create/reuse the Stripe intent outside the transaction with an idempotency key bound to that marker. These prepayment records are immutable quote lines. Only verified settlement may authorize post-payment delivery records. Keep bulk writes and supplier calls out of request/webhook transactions.
2. On verified capture, match the frozen quote, intent, amount and currency, update the parent/ordinal ledger once, and persist the outstanding follow-up in Firestore. Extend the authenticated `app/api/cron/multi-send-dispatch` path to claim and reconcile due wedding jobs in bounded steps, respecting the staging hold. Configure and verify its scheduler on staging. After capture, closing every browser must still allow settlement reconciliation and held status to complete. No work relies on a detached promise after an HTTP response. Show the held purchased envelopes in staging. A webhook replay, concurrent tab, expired reservation or late capture has a deterministic reconciliation path. Never reopen an uncertain intent merely because a lease expired.
3. A child refund reserves against its own frozen refundable components plus parent captured/refunded/reserved counters in a bounded transaction. Complete or release the reservation using a unique refund key and processor evidence. Neither refund requests nor webhooks must read all siblings. Reconcile aggregates asynchronously against child/Stripe evidence.
4. Use a fair queue over eligible parents, bounded child concurrency, time-bounded leases, per-child idempotency and backoff. Quarantine uncertain supplier acceptance for operator reconciliation. Test a large and small project interleaved. Record chunk timing, documents read/written per refund, queue age, fairness, retries and configured limits. Compensating operations keep the captured snapshot and paid ordinal history intact. In staging use held orders and an isolated mock dispatcher; supplier-authorized throughput, scheduler capacity and verified international supplier costs are live-release gates. [Firestore bulk-write and contention guidance](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/best-practices).
