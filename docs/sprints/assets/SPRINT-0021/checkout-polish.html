01 Center the block

Center the full recipient block beneath the sender and stamp. Keep every address line left-aligned and let long content wrap.

02 Reset with intention

Tap the options button to see “Start a new card,” followed by a clear discard confirmation.

03 Honor an empty email

No automatic refill after an edit. Ordinary complete-address orders may omit email. Deferred-address orders need email for the return link.

04 Show what is available

A neutral “Payment” heading. Eligible wallets above card; no guarantee block. Preserve working production Card/Link behavior while correcting staging.
